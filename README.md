<h3 align="center">About Me</h3>

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Toronto (PEY Co-op) with experience in process optimization, AI-driven solutions, and entrepreneurship. Currently, I am working at CIBC as a Process Engineering Analyst, building automation solutions and improving end-to-end processes.

My interests span finance, AI, and business strategy—driven by a vision to bridge technology, analytics, and entrepreneurship into impactful real-world solutions.
